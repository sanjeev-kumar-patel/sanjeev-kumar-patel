## A little about me

I’m a CS student who gets the most out of projects where something is actually broken. Give me a slow query, a weird bug, or a half-working schema and I’ll happily lose a weekend to it. The ones where the answer is sitting in a tutorial three tabs over don’t teach me much.

Right now I’m pulled toward **machine learning and data science**, but I’m doing it the slow way. Before I trust a model’s output, I want to know what’s happening underneath: the probability, the linear algebra, the statistics most tutorials skip. It takes longer. It’s also the only way I end up believing the number at the end.

This is the loop I’m in:

```python
while not convinced:
    loss = how_wrong(model(x))
    grad = derive_by_hand(loss)   # the slow part, on purpose
    x -= learning_rate * grad
```

I turned it into a toy you can poke. Drop a ball on a loss curve and watch it roll downhill (or overshoot): **[sanjeev-kumar-patel.github.io](https://sanjeev-kumar-patel.github.io)**
